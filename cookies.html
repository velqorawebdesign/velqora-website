Cookie Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

In short: this website does not set any cookies and does not use any tracking. That's why you won't see a cookie banner.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites save on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, tracking pixels and device fingerprinting. Under Irish law (the ePrivacy Regulations, S.I. No. 336 of 2011), websites must ask for your consent before using any of these that aren't strictly necessary.

2. What this website uses

Cookies and similar technologies on this website
TypeUsed on this site?
Strictly necessary cookiesNo
Analytics or statistics (for example Google Analytics)No
Advertising or social media tracking pixelsNo
Local storage or session storageNo
Embedded third-party content (videos, maps, social feeds)No
Third-party fonts or scriptsNo, our fonts and code are hosted on this website

Because nothing is stored on or read from your device, no cookie consent is needed.

3. Links to other websites

Our links to Instagram and WhatsApp take you to those services. They only load when you click them, and from then on Meta's own cookie and privacy policies apply.

4. If this changes

If we ever add cookies or analytics, we'll update this policy first. Anything that isn't strictly necessary will only run after you've agreed, through a banner where “Reject” is as easy as “Accept”.

5. Controlling cookies

You can view and delete cookies from any website in your browser's settings. For more about your data, see our Privacy Policy or email velqorawebdesign@gmail.com.
