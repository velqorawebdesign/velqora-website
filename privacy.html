Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

In short: this website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. We only receive personal information that you choose to send us, and we only use it to reply to you and, if you hire us, to deliver your project.

1. Who we are

Velqora is a web design and development business based in Ireland. We are the “controller” of the personal data described in this policy, which means we decide how and why it is used.

  • Email: velqorawebdesign@gmail.com
  • Phone and WhatsApp: +353 89 948 0472
  • Postal address: available on request by email

2. What we collect and why

We follow the principle of data minimisation: we only ask for what we need.

Personal data we process, why, legal basis and how long we keep it
WhatWhyLegal basis (GDPR)How long we keep it
Enquiries: your name, email address or phone number, and whatever you write in your message To reply to you and give you a quote Steps you ask us to take before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) or our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)) Up to 12 months after our last contact if you don't go ahead with a project

The enquiry form

The form on our home page does not send or store anything on this website. When you press the button, it opens your own email app with your message filled in. You choose whether to send it. Only the details you send by email reach us.

What we don't do

  • We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or any tracking. See our Cookie Policy.
  • We don't sell or rent your personal data.
  • We don't send marketing messages unless you've asked us to.
  • We don't use automated decision-making or profiling.

3. Who we share it with

We only share personal data where needed, with:

  • Communication services you choose to use: Google (Gmail) for email, and Meta for WhatsApp and Instagram. They handle your messages under their own privacy policies.
  • Our website hosting provider, which stores the site and its server logs.
  • Our accountant, Irish Revenue or other authorities, where the law requires it.
  • Providers you ask us to set up for your project, such as domain or hosting companies.

4. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers, such as Google and Meta, may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, they rely on recognised safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data deleted
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To use any of these rights, email velqorawebdesign@gmail.com. It's free, and we'll respond within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first.

6. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission:

  • Website: www.dataprotection.ie (opens in a new tab)
  • Address: Data Protection Commission, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28, Ireland

7. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16.

8. Security

We protect your data with secured, password-protected accounts and two-step verification where available, and we only keep what we need for as long as we need it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use personal data, we'll update this page and the “last updated” date above.
