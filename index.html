FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need, such as the number of pages and features. After a quick chat we send a written quote with a fixed price, so you know the cost before anything starts.

How long does it take?

Smaller sites are usually quicker than larger ones. We'll give you a realistic timeline in your quote, based on the scope we agree.

Do you guarantee a top spot on Google?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings. We build your site with solid technical foundations so search engines can read it properly, which gives you the best starting point.

Who owns the website when it's finished?

You do. Once the project is paid in full, the site, its content and the design made for you belong to you. See our Terms & Conditions for details.

Do I need to be in Ireland?

No. We're based in Ireland and work remotely, so we can help wherever you are. We can talk by email, phone, WhatsApp or Instagram.

What if I change my mind?

Read our Refund & Cancellation Policy. If you're a consumer, you may also have a legal 14-day cancellation right.
