Allergen information

My bakes contain or may contain these allergens:

  • Cereals containing gluten (wheat flour)
  • Eggs and milk (sponges and buttercream)
  • Nuts, including hazelnuts (Nutella and Kinder fillings) and pistachio
  • Soya, found in many chocolates and fillings

Everything is made in one home kitchen, so I can't guarantee any bake is free from these allergens. Please tell me about any allergies before you order. The full allergen list for your order is available on request.

How to order

  1. 1. Message me

    Send me a message on Instagram (opens in a new tab) with your order details.

  2. 2. Share your details

    Include:

    • Your name

  6. 6. Enjoy!

    I can't wait for you to enjoy your treats.

    Good to know

    Questions

    How far in advance should I order?

    As early as you can. Every bake is made to order, so message me with your date and I'll let you know if I'm available.

    How do I pay?

    Once I confirm your order and total price, you pay a deposit to secure your date. I'll tell you how to pay, the deposit amount and when the balance is due at that point.

    Where do you deliver?

    Delivery is €10 to Lucan and surrounding areas. You can also collect from me in Lucan. I'll share the collection details when I confirm your order.

    Can you make a design I've seen online?

    Send me your inspiration photos and I'll let you know what I can do and the price. Custom designs, toppers and decorations are priced individually. I'll use them as inspiration to create a design that's your own.

    Can you cater for allergies?

    Please read the allergen information and tell me about any allergies before you order. My kitchen handles gluten, eggs, milk, nuts and soya, so I can't promise any bake is allergen-free.

    What if I need to cancel or change my order?

    Let me know as soon as possible. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for how deposits and refunds work.
