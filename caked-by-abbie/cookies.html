Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

In short: this website doesn't use cookies or tracking, so there's no cookie banner. There's nothing to accept or reject.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can save on your device. Similar tools include local storage, tracking pixels and fingerprinting. Under the ePrivacy Regulations (S.I. No. 336 of 2011), websites must ask for your consent before using any of these that aren't strictly necessary.

2. What this website uses

Cookies and similar technologies on this website
TypeUsed?
Cookies of any kindNo
Analytics (such as Google Analytics)No
Advertising or social media pixels (such as the Meta Pixel)No
Embedded content from other websites (such as an Instagram feed, maps or videos)No
Local storage or session storageNo
Fonts or scripts loaded from other companiesNo. Fonts are stored on this website.

The site's security settings stop the browser loading anything from other websites, so no other company can set cookies through it.

3. Links to Instagram

This website links to Instagram, but doesn't embed it. Nothing from Instagram loads until you click a link. Once you're on Instagram, Meta's own cookie policy applies: Instagram Cookie Policy (opens in a new tab).

4. Changes

If this ever changes, for example by adding analytics, I'll ask for your consent first and update this page before any non-essential cookies are used.
